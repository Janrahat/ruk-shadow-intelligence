Precise GPS coordinates are never requested. This software does not call the browser location API, does not access your camera or microphone, and cannot read your files, contacts or messages.

5. How long it is kept

Analytics records are deleted after {{ notice.retention_days }} days by default. An individual link can specify a shorter window. Consent records are deleted on their own, longer schedule so that a withdrawal can still be honoured later.

6. Your choices

You choose at the notice page whether to allow recording. Declining still takes you to the destination; it simply records nothing. Because no identifier is stored when you decline, there is no record to delete afterwards.

If you allowed recording and want it erased, contact the operator of the site that sent you the link and quote the tracking identifier shown on the notice page. The operator has a one-step erasure control in their dashboard.

7. What this system cannot do

It cannot identify you as a person. It cannot read your passwords, sessions, messages or files. It cannot see anything you do after you leave the destination page.
